What I do [CV]

Collegiate rocketry engineer--specializing in avionics. I'm currently an electrical engineering, mathematics, and physics undergraduate student at UC San Diego. I'm also a hardware engineer for avionics at the Rocket Propulsion Laboratory. My passion lies within the realm of theory to describe the universe in a rigorous mathematical language. My philosophy as an engineer, an experimentalist, is to deconstruct components as their built to the most discrete level then assemble it again from the ground up, in-house. We aim to study physics as an engineer to create analog and digital elements. A theoretical understanding always enables practical applications; A direct pipeline from physics to engineering is clear.

My experience encompasses both software and hardware, including soldering, 3D printing, mechanical CAD (OnShape, Fusion, Blender), electrical CAD (KiCAD, PSpice), robotics assembly, breadboard assembly (Arduino, ESP32), and high-level computer programming languages in C and MATLAB.

I have launched a total of [2] rockets in my career thus far. One with a G-class motor and one two-stage I-class rocket, both under Rocket Propulsion Laboratory at UC San Diego. As my lifetime as an engineer grows, I dawn new ideas for staging and recovery actuation, and telemetry.
